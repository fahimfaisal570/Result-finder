- **Learnings for future iterations:**
  - Window functions (`ROW_NUMBER()`) are essential when "first chronologically" semantics differ from "minimum/maximum value" semantics. Using `MAX(CASE WHEN rn_asc = 1 THEN grade_point END)` cleanly extracts the first-window value in a single pass.
  - Civil Engineering uses consecutive semester numbers starting from 1 per year rather than resetting to 1. A simple threshold check (`sem_in_yr > 2`) correctly distinguishes consecutive numbering from standard bi-semester cycles.
  - Bounding computed metrics (like consistency scores) to valid ranges (`>= 0.0`) prevents downstream rendering bugs caused by negative mathematical results.
  - Filtering out unparseable semester data (`sem_num=0`) is critical when performing mathematical regression, as a data point at origin zero will severely skew slope calculations.
  - Avoid defining caching functions inside conditional rendering blocks or functions in Streamlit; module-level caching is much cleaner, reliable, and prevents warnings or repeated definitions.
